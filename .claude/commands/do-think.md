Think this through with me. Do not plan it, do not write a file, do not
edit code.

The thought: $ARGUMENTS

This is a conversation, and it comes before any plan. A plan written
from a half-formed idea is a plan for something I did not want. Your job
here is to find out what I actually want.

1. **Say it back first.** In two or three plain sentences, tell me what
   you understand me to be asking for and why you think I want it. Then
   ask: "Is that right?" Stop and wait. Do not go further until I confirm
   or correct you. If I correct you, say it back again.

2. **Then talk it through with me.** One question at a time, the most
   important first. Wait for each answer. Ask about things like:
   - what a person using the app would see or do differently
   - what is wrong with how it is today, in my words
   - where it starts and where it stops: what is not part of this
   - anything that could be read two ways
   - what "done" would look like to me
   Give me your own view when it helps: if two ways of doing it differ in
   a way I would care about, say which you would pick and why, in plain
   language. If my idea conflicts with something the app already does or a
   rule in CLAUDE.md, tell me. If it is a bad idea, say so and say why.

3. **Look only when you must.** Read a file only when a question cannot
   be answered without it, and keep it to that file. This is thinking, not
   exploring. No tests, no builds.

4. **Don't rush to finish.** Keep going until neither of us has an open
   question, or until I say "that's it". A few exchanges is normal. Do not
   wrap up after one answer.

5. **Close by handing over.** When it is settled, give me:
   - **What we agreed**, in a short list, in my terms.
   - **What we left out**, on purpose.
   - **Still open**, if anything, and who decides it.
   - **The line to run next**, in a code block, ready to paste:
     `/do-all <one paragraph that carries everything we agreed, the
     limits, and what done looks like>`
   Then stop. Do not run `/do-all` or `/do-plan` yourself and do not
   start planning, even if I say "great" or "go ahead": tell me to
   paste the line.

Keep every message short. Plain language, no jargon, no file names unless
I ask.
